About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Old Dominion University, advised by Dr. Jian Wu, and expect to graduate in December 2027. My dissertation, “Toward Preserving Open Access Datasets and Software in Scholarly Documents”, measures the availability and discoverability of open access datasets and software (OADS) URLs at scale and how fast they decline.

Authors point to the datasets and software their studies use through URLs in their papers, which I call OADS‑URLs. Many no longer lead to the resource they point to, and how much of the cited OADS is still reachable is not well understood. This leaves web archives no basis for deciding which endangered resources to preserve first.

We built two benchmarks, sURL‑3K and OADS‑1K. On sURL‑3K, our proposed extraction method, VisURLX, reads rendered page images with a multimodal large language model (MLLM) and achieves an F1 score of 86.86%. On OADS‑1K, EnSU classifies each URL by resource type and provider at a macro F1 of 90%. With these methods, I am building OADS Repo from 16.1M scholarly documents.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a software engineer at Nascenia for two years. I have also interned at Los Alamos National Laboratory and the Virginia Marine Resources Commission.

Research

Contextualized URL Extraction

CodePaper

86.86% end-to-end F1, 19–40 points above GROBID, olmOCR, and PyMuPDF pipelines

  • Created sURL‑3K, the first benchmark for evaluating URL and context extraction, with 3,376 manually annotated URLs and their contexts from 352 scholarly papers.
  • Built VisURLX, an MLLM-based pipeline that reads rendered page images and extracts each URL with its context across the body, footnotes, and references, restoring footnote and reference entries into the sentences that cite them.
  • Presented the first systematic comparison of image-based and text-based extraction across accuracy, monetary cost, and computational cost, using proprietary and open-weight backends.

MLLMs, GPT-5-mini, Qwen3-VL-32B, Hugging Face Transformers, SLURM

OADS‑URL Classification

CodePaper

90% macro F1, 15–22 points above few-shot GPT-4 and Claude baselines

  • Created OADS‑1K, a benchmark for evaluating OADS‑URL classification, with 1,129 manually annotated URLs and their contexts from 1,574 scholarly documents.
  • Developed EnSU, an ensemble of fine-tuned language models that assigns each URL one of six fine-grained classes from its context, crossing resource type (dataset or software) with provider (author or third party).

SciBERT, BertGCN, contrastive learning, PyTorch

Preserving Open Access Datasets and Software

Dissertation, in progress

16.1M scholarly documents

  • Building OADS Repo, a corpus of OADS‑URLs from 16.1M scholarly documents, using a hybrid approach combining VisURLX and GROBID for contextualized URL extraction and EnSU for classifying them.
  • Measuring how availability and discoverability differ across discipline, provider, host, venue, and institution, and how fast OADS‑URLs disappear.

VisURLX, EnSU, GROBID, AWS (S3)
